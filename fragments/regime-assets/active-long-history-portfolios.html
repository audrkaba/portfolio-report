Model context and methodology

Active history rule: 70 eligible assets; 1127 training observations (2021-11-29–2026-05-13) and 126 separate diagnostic observations (2026-05-15–2026-11-09). Short-history instruments are excluded from decisions and the dominance reference set. All 12 current holdings remain available. Fund NAV already includes ongoing fees.

126-day segment is a descriptive fixed-allocation diagnostic, not an independent strategy backtest: current forecast centres use data through their current origin. Official data refreshed 2026-11-11T08:17:37.435296+02:00: share closes through 2026-11-10; latest available EUR fund NAV dates 2026-11-09. Each profile states its own observation date. Weekly models, joint scenarios, technical signals and allocations are refitted from this snapshot. The new canonical netSD reference network contains the 70 eligible assets and Current, with one training-derived denominator. Old comparison policies containing excluded holdings are not silently projected or backfilled. The S&P 500 benchmark uses USD price returns; currency and dividend bases differ from EUR assets, and benchmark measures use only matching US trading dates.

Maximum common history: 1253 aligned daily returns; the earliest 1127 form the training sample and the latest 126 form the diagnostic segment. No earlier common observations were discarded. Investor exclusions: AIR, EJTC, FRGTE, MAGIC, SE0029504588. Ineligible shorter histories are retained in the private archive.

Historical portfolio series apply constant origin-price weights to daily asset returns: they are hypothetical daily-rebalanced diagnostics, not realised account histories or buy-and-hold share-quantity backtests. The future scenario policy instead tracks integer holdings and cash through its two decision stages. — denotes a native R function that is undefined for the selected sample; no replacement ratio is invented.

Purchase signal restrictions removed (5 November 2026): both stages may purchase without technical BUY votes and without a positive conditional remaining forecast. Purchases are chosen by the scenario-expected netSD minus penalties objective. The EUR 750 initial additional purchase limit per asset, EUR 15,000 total budget, integer lots, zero to four funds, history and liquidity restrictions, and zero-interest cash remain. Technical indicators remain diagnostics. Recourse sales still require a negative conditional forecast and at least 3 SELL votes; the no-sales policy prohibits all sales. There are twenty independent scenario-specific recourse vectors. The initial plan is common, and the complete representative scenario is assumed revealed at the second-stage date.

Confirmed actual holdings: last confirmed 2026-11-11T10:12:28.271346+00:00. Current quantities are actual holdings; unexecuted model purchases are shown separately as targets. Valuations use the report market snapshot. Actual execution prices were not supplied, so portfolio values are not presented as transaction costs or realised account profit.

Coincident historical curves: the main and no-sales policies have identical first-stage quantities in this snapshot. Their historical return series and performance measures therefore coincide. Their node-specific recourse holdings and expected scenario scores differ; compare tables 1.4.c–e. Historical curves describe the initial holdings, not future realised recourse.
