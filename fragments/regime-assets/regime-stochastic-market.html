The scenario model reuses the published historical four-regime transition matrix. Its initial state is the filtered HMM classification at the forecast origin. Historical calibration pools use only pre-origin retrospective labels. This is an origin-calibrated forecast, not a held-out investment test.

All five Baltic indices enter an equal-weight log-return factor; MSCI World EUR gross return is the global factor. Native R regressions connect these common market movements to all 86 forecast-covered reference assets. Forecast coverage is distinct from purchase eligibility: funds must also have five years of verified share-class history. Forecast methods have equal unconditional probabilities; conditional probabilities update after an observed price prefix.
