3.8.1. Design, information cutoffs and execution assumptions

Each historical origin refits daily and weekly models, factor regressions, HMM regimes and transition probabilities using only its prefix. Forecasts share a Thursday origin and a 28-calendar-day endpoint. The most recent 12 fully completed predictions choose models and ensemble weights; no evaluation outcome enters its own selection. Daily/weekly source families receive 50/50 prior mass and form five joint-asset clusters, four observable two-day nodes and 80 future leaves. Latent future cluster or regime identities are never used to select the realised recourse action.

Portfolio calibration uses available-quote returns, including the assumed NAV lag, and starts with at least 1000 observed Baltic-session returns and expands at every origin. The full subsequent account path is held out from that decision. Root and recourse orders execute mechanically at the next close / next-session observed fund NAV, subject to cash, whole units, new-stock lots, fund entry sizes, EUR 750 initial additional purchases per asset and at most 20% of observed daily share volume. An order without a genuine next-session fund valuation is unfilled; forward-filled valuations are not invented execution prices. Recourse sale signals are recomputed from the actually observed two-day prefix and the previously forecast conditional endpoint. The same production objective and constraints are searched with the fixed 32-iteration heuristic; no global optimality certificate is claimed. A failed feasible-start search explicitly holds existing positions.

Fund NAV publication timestamps are unavailable: information uses an assumed two-business-day lag. External peer inputs are additionally lagged. The modelling grid carries already observed prices across weekday market closures; this is an approximation to historical exchange calendars. Zero-interest cash, gross cash dividends and unpaid dividend receivables are included. Unverified payment dates remain locked receivables rather than spendable cash. Dividend-announcement coverage starts in 2023; the inherited dividend penalty uses the available past payout sum with its fixed five-year scaling, not a claim of complete pre-2023 dividend history. Personal taxes and order-book impact are not reconstructed.

The 0/10/25/50 bp execution-cost stress cases replay the same signed zero-commission executed trade quantities with independent cash-constrained fills; they are not separately cost-aware optimised strategies. The primary 0 bp setting reflects zero commissions and is an optimistic execution benchmark. Cost stresses represent possible spread/implementation effects, not broker fees. Current buy and hold starts with the same hypothetical EUR 15000 account, initial quantities and cash as the tested strategies.

Descriptive rank stability, not a formal significance test. Paired circular blocks resample all asset and realised-strategy returns together. The complete asset-plus-baseline dominance reference network and its denominator are recomputed for every resample. The central 95% ranges describe sensitivity of a discontinuous ranking; they are not asserted confidence intervals. Fractions of positive differences are not p-values. This conditions on realised strategy paths and does not refit the complete trading strategy inside every bootstrap sample.
