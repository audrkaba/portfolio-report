Reuses the existing cleaned price/NAV histories. HMM regimes are assigned only on exactly matching dates. Full and Last 126 retain all available return dates; regime columns omit dates without a common market label. Regime definitions use the full-history HMM fit and are descriptive, not an independent investment backtest.

Path measures: drawdown, recovery and cumulative-return measures in regime columns use a synthetic sequence of the selected regime observations in chronological order, with other days omitted. They are not the actual drawdown or realised investment return across the omitted days. Annualisation uses 252 selected observations, not elapsed calendar time. Benchmark measures use exact-date pairs with S&P 500 USD price returns; currency and dividend bases differ.
