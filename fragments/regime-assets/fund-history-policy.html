Minimum verified fund history: five calendar years. At the frozen analysis origin 2026-10-30, 17 of 24 funds qualify and 7 are excluded from new investment. This is a hard zero-quantity restriction at both stages, not an objective penalty.

Eligibility uses the later of the first available NAV and the exact share-class inception date. Older predecessor/linked performance does not count. The latest verified NAV must also be current within seven calendar days of the analysis origin. Whole years are assessed by calendar dates, not by rounding a displayed age.

The main signal-gated policy and the regime-conditioned policy already hold only qualifying funds at both stages. Their saved first-stage quantities remain feasible. WS candidates and forecast-method grouping are re-solved under the stricter bounds; earlier WS candidates containing excluded funds are not reused as admissible solutions.
