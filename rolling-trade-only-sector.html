Approved sector rule and this research revision

Unused cash is allowed; no minimum purchase is required. Price changes may passively push a sector above30%. A new transaction must keep each sector at or below30% of account wealth, or its existing marked EUR exposure if that is already larger. Holdings and cash can be retained without compulsory sales. Existing sale permissions and all other model restrictions remain. Both C4 and Full are reoptimised under this same rule, using matched frozen C4 warm starts by default. A failed numerical start may be preserved and replaced by Current; the paired Full case then uses the same Current start. Case-level recovery provenance records these exceptions. Dated study inventory and market endpoint remain fixed for comparability.

Earlier completed strict-sector study is archived separately. Its infeasibility findings do not apply to this revised contract. Only checked revised origins are shown here.

Numerical integrity: inconsistent vectorised terminal-wealth rows are independently reconstructed with 50-digit decimal products and sums, cross-checked using scalar math.fsum at absolute return tolerance 1e-12. The complete saved policy must still pass the unchanged independent node and terminal-outcome validator. A numerical recovery is not a global-optimality certificate.

Methodology and comparability

The extension restores each original saved full training tree before C4 reduction. Forecasts and HMM estimates are reused exactly from that origin; future evaluation observations never enter optimisation. Estimation spans six calendar months, OOS one calendar month, origins advance weekly, and a per-origin held-out tail extends to the frozen data endpoint2026-11-17. Initial capital is EUR15,000.

Full branching20×20×20×20 produces160,000 terminal outcomes and8,421 decisions including the root; C4 branching20×20×10×4 produces16,000 outcomes and4,421 decisions. The full tree has10 times as many outcomes and about1.905 times as many decision nodes. These dimension ratios are not measured speed-up factors.

The original frozen research inventory and sales convention are retained. Later production holdings and US-sale exceptions are not introduced into this paired extension. Same costs and observed-prefix execution apply; the sector rule is replaced by the approved trade-only rule described above. Passive exposures above30% are disclosed and permitted. Heuristic optimisation and tree discretisation can both affect attained solutions; no global optimum is claimed.

Completed OOS comparisons use paired mean differences on aligned weekly origins, centred circular block bootstrap with19,999 replications (primary block30 origins; sensitivity4,8,13,26,52), marginal95% basic intervals, Bartlett HAC cross-checks and Holm adjustment across every contrast and nine outcomes within the stated family and population. Common-case and pairwise-available coverage remain separate. These exploratory tests require weak dependence and sufficiently stable finite moments; structural changes and short-window tail ratios limit conclusions. Missing weekly positions remain missing rather than zero.

Final wealth is cash plus marked positions at the selected endpoint. OOS ends at the monthly test endpoint; HELD_OUT ends at the frozen data endpoint, without resetting account wealth. Means describe separate origin accounts. Overlapping monthly returns are never multiplied together. IS is a rebased fitted-allocation diagnostic.
