Interpretation and methods

All 100 weighted-sum settings are retained, λ = 0,…,1. λ = 0 gives the entire objective weight to normalised netSD; λ = 1 gives it to normalised expected terminal account return. Intermediate settings change this objective balance. The epsilon method instead uses a return-floor index α and an IS-calibrated expected-return floor ε; these are not λ. Equal numeric indices do not imply equal preferences.

Each setting optimises a discrete feasible portfolio, so several settings can select identical holdings. Repeated solutions remain visible in the grid and composition charts; 100 settings need not yield 100 distinct Pareto portfolios. A heuristic search can also return dominated points. The attained nondominated envelope is identified explicitly. Connecting lines are visual guides, not attainable fractional interpolations or a global optimality certificate.

Portfolios are fitted on IS forecasts and scenarios. Their realised OOS outcomes are then reported for every setting, without using outer OOS to select λ. The OOS chart carries the same IS-fitted policies; it is not an ex-ante OOS Pareto optimisation. Inner chronological preference selection is an additional comparison, not a replacement for the full front.

Weights are initial account weights: fitted target units × origin price / EUR15,000, including cash. They describe the first decision. Conditional later-stage decisions remain in the fitted complete policy and its OOS replay. Cash is shown explicitly; these weights are distinct from weights normalised over invested assets alone.

IS/OOS correlations — all models, windows and preference settings

Pearson and Spearman cross-matrices compare all available IS performance measures and numerical decision components with realised OOS measures. Matrices are separate for each model/method/constraint group. Within-window100-setting correlations are descriptive; repeated portfolios are not independent observations. Across-window correlations are evaluated at a fixed preference index. Constant components have undefined correlations, shown as blank cells rather than zero. Nested-selected same-metric comparisons additionally use2000 calendar-block bootstrap replicates; overlapping monthly tests are not independent.
