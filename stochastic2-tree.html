Regimes and computational limitations

R1: Baltic and global markets calm; R2: Baltic stress; R3: global stress; R4: stress in both market groups. K1–K5 are five shared clusters of weekly forecast price paths. The conditional 20-state model is estimated from the earlier 12,000 joint simulations; rare transitions are smoothed and regime probabilities retain the earlier transition matrix. Cluster means smooth extreme outcomes. Integer search is heuristic without a global-optimality certificate. Final GPU scenario calculations are independently verified on CPU.
