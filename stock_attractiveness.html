Method and limitations

Provisional weights: valuation 45%, business quality 25%, technical condition 20%, official-disclosure tone 10%. Combined scores average available components using weight multiplied by component coverage. These weights and thresholds have not been calibrated or validated out of sample. Missing disclosure evidence is not assigned a neutral 50.

Valuation averages sector-relative ranks of three-year median annual EPS divided by the snapshot price, and annual equity divided by annual average diluted shares and the snapshot price. Both scored yields therefore use the latest research quote. The second ratio is a book-yield proxy, not an exact current P/B: average annual share counts may differ from current outstanding shares. Counts below 5 million are excluded because provider rounding creates excessive uncertainty. Provider P/E and P/B are displayed only as diagnostics and never substituted into the score. Ranks shrink towards 50 with smaller peer samples. Sector groups with fewer than four stocks fall back to a broader financial/non-financial group; the three banks remain separate with limited confidence. Unvalidated weights and annual proxies cap confidence at Moderate. Negative earnings and equity are never interpreted as cheap positive multiples. Per-share historical figures may be affected by corporate actions. No discounted-cash-flow fair-value claim is made.

Quality uses three-year median ROE, cash conversion, debt/equity and three-year revenue growth. Financial firms instead use ROE, ROA and growth; industrial leverage penalties are not applied to banks. Negative equity invalidates ROE and caps quality at 20. Regulatory capital, non-performing loans, financial-statement restatements and recent interim results are not fully covered.

Technical condition averages inverse RSI, inverse Bollinger position and standardized distance from the 200-observation mean within one temperature family (80%), plus the 50/200-observation trend (20%). Correlated oscillators are grouped, not counted as independent signals. TradingView ratings are not added again. Sparse trading reduces confidence.

Disclosure tone is a conservative rule-based proxy from up to three substantive official issuer releases in the last 90 days, with a 30-day half-life. Profit direction carries more weight than revenue direction. Mixed or unrecognized language remains unclassified. This is not full market sentiment: social media, analyst consensus, earnings surprises, acquisition-adjusted organic growth and price reaction are absent. Tone coverage is capped at 60%. Positive sales news alone cannot establish undervaluation.

Financial statements and market prices have different dates; fiscal year labels do not establish a release date. This is a current exploratory snapshot, unsuitable for a historical backtest without point-in-time filing availability. Provider P/B date uncertainty, rounded financials and peer comparability constrain confidence. The indicator does not alter portfolio inputs, constraints or optimization.
