Data timing: external quotes come from TradingView and can be delayed. The D label in a widget indicates delayed data; a paid TradingView account does not automatically make embedded website data real-time. TradingView data FAQ.

Periods: last week opens TradingView's 5D range, last year opens the 12-month range, and today opens the latest intraday session supplied by TradingView. When the market is closed or no newer trades are available, the last available session remains visible. Sparse candles reflect the provider's actual trading data; no trades are invented to fill gaps.

Report calculations: this preview does not refresh the portfolio model, technical signals or forecast snapshot. Fund charts will only be added if the exact fund share class has a verified TradingView symbol; no substitute ETF is used.
