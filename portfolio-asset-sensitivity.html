Portfolio asset sensitivity tables

Asset-level sensitivity — Current, initial purchases and later maximum purchases

Each row changes only one asset. Other holdings or model trades stay fixed as stated below; cash proceeds and purchase costs are retained. Metrics use the complete original four-week forecast law, not a weighted average of individual asset scores.

A. All held assets — sell the entire position
B. Recommended initial purchases — exact first-stage quantity
C. Later purchases — maximum gross quantity attained on one scenario path
Definitions, baseline choices and interpretation

Values show the portfolio parameter after the counterfactual. Δ is after minus the stated baseline; percentage-valued metrics use percentage-point changes. Hover a value to inspect its exact before value. Sharpe and Sortino use zero risk-free return/target and are unannualised four-week ratios. Rachev uses best5% mean divided by the negative worst5% mean. CVaR95 is the negative weighted worst5% return, including fractional probability at the tail boundary. Undefined ratios remain unavailable.

Current liquidation sells all actually held units in the chosen inventory snapshot, keeps the cash and changes no other Current position. The optional selected-model liquidation cancels this asset’s initial target and all its later trades; every other selected-model trade remains. Removal is a hypothetical sale even under No-sale.

Initial-buy rows include only strictly positive proposed initial increments. They compare the selected initial portfolio with the same portfolio missing just this buy. Later rows include only strictly positive later purchases on a positive-probability path; absent assets and zero purchases are omitted.

For each later maximum, sum gross positive purchases across that asset’s complete path, select an actual schedule attaining the maximum, then replay that same signed schedule over every original path at the corresponding week’s prices. Among tied schedules choose the most probable complete schedule, with deterministic ties; never select by realised return or mix incompatible per-stage maxima. Exact initial trades and later sales remain. Other assets keep their original model trades.

No portfolio is reoptimised. A cash or negative-holding violation on any positive-probability path makes the counterfactual unavailable; paths are never dropped. These sensitivity diagnostics are not constraint-projected executable orders: sector, stage caps, lot sizes and sale permissions can also prevent execution. Stochastic/StochA use20 original terminal outcomes; Stochastic2/stoch2A and static allocations use160000. Compare each row only with its matched forecast-law baseline. The network uses all71 asset references plus matched Current/cash and recomputes the candidate-dependent denominator.
